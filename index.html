Trade Policy Activity Index

Trade Policy Activity Index

A novel composite indicator measuring the evolution of global trade policy since the Global Financial Crisis, covering 197 countries and territories.

About the TPA Index

The Trade Policy Activity (TPA) Index is a novel monthly indicator of global trade policy dynamics covering 197 economies since the Global Financial Crisis, spanning January 2010–October 2025 in the original release, with updated data to be made available through this website. Methodological details are available in the underlying paper (Centorrino et al., 2025).

How has it been constructed?

The TPA Index is built using a Dynamic Factor Model applied to records of a wide range of policy measures — including tariffs, non-tariff measures, and subsidies — drawn from two complementary sources: the WTO Trade Monitoring Database and the Global Trade Alert. The model treats these records as imperfect signals of latent global trade policy activity and extracts a parsimonious global trade policy factor capturing their common dynamics. A trend-cycle decomposition distinguishes long-term shifts from cyclical fluctuations, while a block structure separates the global signal from category-specific variation.

What does it include?

The dataset provides a headline Trade Policy Activity Index — a monthly global measure running from January 2010 (through October 2025 in the original release, with periodic updates planned) — together with a set of sub-indices by policy category and country group. Separate series track facilitating measures and non-facilitating measures, with the latter further split into a restrictive subset and a residual composed largely of subsidies. The index is also estimated separately for G20 and non-G20 economies, alongside a GDP-weighted variant. All series are normalized to mean zero over January 2010–December 2011.
